    Onefinity Redline Adapter¶

    For the Onefinity Gen 2 machines that ship with the RealTime CNC RTS-2 controller. The adapter carries the same Molex connector families as the RTS-2, so the machine's harness plugs straight in. Pinouts follow the published RTS-2 I/O reference.

    Status

    Designed from RealTime CNC's published pinout. Not yet verified on a machine.

    Connectors¶

    Qty Function Connector on the adapter Mates with
    5 Motor, X Y1 Y2 Z A Mini-Fit Jr 2×4 vertical The machine's motor cable, 46992-0810
    5 Homing sensor Mini-Fit Jr 1×3 vertical 50-29-1662
    1 Probe Micro-Fit 3.0 1×2 43025-0200
    1 Tool setter Micro-Fit 3.0 1×2 43025-0200
    1 PWM, laser or spindle Micro-Fit 3.0 1×2 43025-0200
    1 VFD RS-485 Micro-Fit 3.0 2×2 43025-0400
    2 Flood, mist Micro-Fit 3.0 1×2 43025-0200
    1 To the mainboard 64-pin keyed box header, ribbon ncCore ADAPTER header
    1 Motor bus XT30 male ncCore MOTOR PWR

    The Redline PSU plugs into the mainboard's power input, which has the same Mini-Fit 3+3 pinout.

    Motor connector, per axis¶

    Pin Signal From
    1 GND Field ground
    2 STEP+ Axis step +
    3 DIR+ Axis direction +
    4 EN ENA_P, single-ended enable. ENA_N is not connected on the adapter
    5 VM Motor bus from the XT30
    6 STEP− Axis step −
    7 DIR− Axis direction −
    8 FAULT Axis alarm −. The alarm + is tied to the fused 5 V on the adapter, so the driver's open-collector FAULT sinks the opto LED. Low is healthy

    Enable polarity is set with $4 in grblHAL. FAULT active-low is the default alarm polarity.

    Homing sensor, per axis¶

    Pin Signal
    1 GND
    2 Fused 5 V
    3 Limit input

    The Redline sensors run on 5 V, so set LIMIT V on the mainboard to 5 V.

    Probe, tool setter, PWM¶

    Two pins each: the signal and ground. The probe common is fixed at 5 V on the mainboard.

    RS-485¶

    Pins 1 A, 2 B, 3 GND, 4 GND. Fit the RS485 TERM jumper on the mainboard.

    Flood and mist¶

    Two pins each: the fused 24 V and the switched return.

    Not on the adapter¶

    The RTS-2's DB-25 aux port is covered by the mainboard's own aux I/O terminals. The E-stop is the mainboard's panel terminal; since the Redline's own E-stop cuts the PSU, fit the BYPASS jumper.
